About Ātma

Where ancient tradition meets modern life

Ātma Yoga is more than a studio — it's a sanctuary for those seeking balance, strength, and inner peace. Our approach honors the roots of yoga while adapting to the rhythm of contemporary life.

Every class is an invitation to listen inward, move with intention, and connect with a community that uplifts.
